Finance called: at month-end, the gateway total and our invoice total differed by 147 units.

Small amount, but in accounting "almost right" means "wrong".

Three mistakes caused it:
1. double for money. 0.1 + 0.2 is 0.30000000000000004, and thousands of lines accumulate the error.
2. Rounding in different places with different rules. Math.Round defaults to banker's rounding (2.5 becomes 2), accounting usually wants AwayFromZero.
3. Splitting 100 between 3 people without deciding who gets the remainder: 33.33 x 3 = 99.99.

The fix: decimal (or minor units as long), one explicit rounding policy, remainder allocation, and a Money value object that carries the currency.

In a financial system, one wrong cent is a bug that has not been discovered yet.
