It was 10 AM on a Thursday when the first alert fired: database CPU at 95%.

No new deploy. Just a sales campaign that tripled traffic.

Everyone said: "Scale up the database."

The traces said something else: one request to the orders API ran 201 SQL queries. Lazy loading. 10 rows in dev (21 queries, 120 ms). 100 rows in production, thousands of requests per minute.

The fix was ~10 lines: one projected query. CPU went from 95% to 18%. No scale-up.

What I took from it:
1. Small test data hides scale bugs, so assert query counts in tests.
2. Turn lazy loading off.
3. Log SQL in dev.
4. Count queries before buying hardware.

Where have you found an N+1?
