The order was saved, but the confirmation email never went out.

The database and the message broker don't share a transaction. If the process crashes between SaveChanges and Publish, the two systems drift apart and nobody gets an error.

The fix is the Transactional Outbox:
1. Save the message in an OutboxMessages table, in the same transaction as the order
2. A background worker reads it and publishes it
3. Mark it as sent

The guarantee is at-least-once delivery, so consumers must be idempotent (the Inbox pattern).

Tools: MassTransit Outbox, CAP, Wolverine.
