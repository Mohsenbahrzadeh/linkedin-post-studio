Exceptions or Result?

Exceptions are for the exceptional. When a failure is expected — not found, invalid input, a rule that didn't pass — model it as data and return a Result<T>. The failure lives in the signature, callers must handle it, and you compose without try/catch on the happy path.

Still throw for broken invariants, truly unexpected I/O, or anything this layer can't sensibly handle.

Do you return Result or throw? Where do you draw the line?
